Admission Tests:
For each group, there will be separate
Admission Tests. Each admission test will be
conducted in two phases in one sitting as
follows. There will be an MCQ exam followed
by a written exam. However, for each group,
the scripts for the written exam will be
evaluated only if the score in the MCQ exam is
higher than or equal to a cut-off set by the
Admission Committee. The decision of the
Admission Committee in this regard will be
final. The Final Merit List for each group will
be determined by summing the scores of the
MCQ and written exams. The duration of the
MCQ Exam will be 50 minutes, and that of
the written exam will be 1 hour. The written
exam will consist of 10 questions (100 marks),
and the MCQ exam will consist of 50
questions (50 marks). There will be negative
marks in the MCQ exam: 0.5 marks will be
deducted for each wrong answer. Only non
programmable
